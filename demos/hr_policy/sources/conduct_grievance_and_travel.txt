MERIDIAN MARITIME - CONDUCT, GRIEVANCE AND BUSINESS TRAVEL
Policy HR-310, effective 1 January 2026

1. Code of conduct
Meridian Maritime has zero tolerance for harassment, bullying and discrimination. Gifts or hospitality with a value above 50 USD from suppliers or customers must be declared to the Compliance Officer. Any conflict of interest must be disclosed to the line manager within 14 days of the employee becoming aware of it.

2. Raising a grievance
Step 1: the employee should first try to resolve the matter informally with their line manager within 10 working days of the incident.
Step 2: if unresolved, the employee submits a written complaint to HR within 15 working days of the incident. HR acknowledges the complaint within 2 working days and completes an investigation within 20 working days.
Step 3: the employee may appeal the outcome to the Director within 7 days of receiving it. The Director's decision is final.

3. Disciplinary process
Disciplinary action follows four stages: a verbal warning, a written warning, a final written warning and dismissal. A written warning remains valid for 12 months and a final written warning for 18 months. Gross misconduct, such as theft or violence, may lead directly to dismissal.

4. Whistleblowing
Concerns about fraud, safety or serious misconduct can be reported confidentially to ethics@meridianmaritime.example or to the anonymous ethics line on 0800 555 0142. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is a disciplinary offence.

5. Business travel
Flights shorter than 6 hours are booked in economy class; flights of 6 hours or longer may be booked in premium economy. The maximum hotel rate is 140 USD per night. The daily meal allowance is 45 USD. Employees must submit expense claims with receipts within 14 days of returning from travel. Claims submitted later than 14 days are not reimbursed unless the Finance Director approves an exception.

6. Use of company equipment
Company laptops and phones may be used for limited personal purposes. Employees must not install unlicensed software. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
