MERIDIAN MARITIME - WORKING HOURS AND OVERTIME POLICY
Policy HR-204, effective 1 January 2026

1. Standard working week
The standard working week for shore staff is 40 hours, Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 17:00 with an unpaid 30 minute lunch break. Core hours, when everyone is expected to be available, are 10:00 to 15:00. Staff who will arrive after 09:30 must inform their line manager.

2. Overtime
Overtime must be approved in advance by the line manager. Unapproved overtime is not paid. Approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate on weekdays and at 2 times the normal hourly rate on Sundays and public holidays. No employee may work more than 10 hours of overtime in a single week.

Instead of payment, an employee may choose compensatory time off at a rate of one hour off for each overtime hour worked. Compensatory time off must be taken within 60 days of the overtime being worked.

3. Night work
Hours worked between 22:00 and 06:00 attract a night premium of 20 percent on top of the normal hourly rate. The night premium and the overtime rate are not added together; the higher of the two applies.

4. On-call duty
Staff on the 24-hour operations rota receive an on-call allowance of 75 USD per week. If called out, the employee is paid for a minimum of 2 hours at the overtime rate, even if the work takes less time.

5. Remote working
After completing probation, employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with their line manager's agreement. Remote working days must be recorded in the HR portal by the end of the previous week. Roles that need physical presence in the operations room are not eligible for remote working.

6. Timesheets
Timesheets must be submitted by the 25th of each month. Late timesheets are paid in the following month's payroll.
