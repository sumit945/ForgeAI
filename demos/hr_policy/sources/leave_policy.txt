MERIDIAN MARITIME - LEAVE POLICY (SHORE STAFF)
Policy HR-101, effective 1 January 2026

1. Annual leave
Full-time shore staff are entitled to 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 working days per month. Part-time staff accrue leave pro rata to their contracted hours.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they lapse without payment.

Employees in their probation period (the first 6 months of employment) accrue leave normally but may take no more than 5 days of leave during probation.

2. Requesting leave
Requests of 5 or more consecutive working days require at least 10 working days' notice. Shorter absences require at least 2 working days' notice. The line manager must approve or decline within 3 working days of the request. Leave is requested through the HR portal.

3. Public holidays
Shore staff receive 11 public holidays per year. The list of dates is published by HR each December for the following year. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, no replacement day is given.

4. Sick leave
Employees receive 12 paid sick days per calendar year. Staff must inform their line manager before 09:30 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required once an absence exceeds 3 consecutive working days. Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out.

5. Parental leave
Birth mothers receive 26 weeks of maternity leave on full pay. Fathers and partners receive 4 weeks of paternity leave on full pay, which must be taken within 6 months of the birth. Adoptive parents receive 12 weeks of leave on full pay.

6. Bereavement leave
Employees receive 5 days of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, partner, child, parent, sibling) and 2 days for an extended family member.

7. Unpaid leave
After 2 years of service an employee may request up to 30 days of unpaid leave in a calendar year. Unpaid leave must be approved in writing by the Director of the employee's department.
