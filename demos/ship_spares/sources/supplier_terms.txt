SUPPLIER TERMS OF TRADE - APPROVED SPARES SUPPLIERS
Maintained by Fleet Procurement, revision 7, March 2026

1. Harbourline Marine Supply (supplier code HLM)
Payment terms are net 30 days from invoice date. An order discount of 5 percent applies to orders with a value above 5,000 USD before freight. Freight is free for orders above 2,500 USD; for smaller orders a flat freight charge of 120 USD applies to any port.

Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery. A restocking fee of 15 percent of the item value is charged on returned items. Emergency orders, dispatched within 48 hours, carry a surcharge of 25 percent of the item value. All Harbourline parts carry a 12 month warranty from the date of delivery.

2. Northwind Engineering (supplier code NWE)
Payment terms are net 45 days from invoice date. An order discount of 3 percent applies to orders with a value above 10,000 USD before freight. Freight is a flat 250 USD to any port, regardless of order value.

Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery, and unopened items are returned without a restocking fee; opened items incur a 20 percent restocking fee. Express orders, dispatched within 24 hours, carry a surcharge of 40 percent of the item value. Engine parts carry a 24 month warranty from delivery; all other Northwind parts carry a 12 month warranty.

3. Pacific Valve and Pump (supplier code PVP)
Payment terms are net 60 days from invoice date. No volume discount is offered. Freight is charged at cost, quoted per order. Returns are not accepted on special-order items. Standard items may be returned within 21 days with a 10 percent restocking fee. All Pacific Valve and Pump parts carry an 18 month warranty from delivery.

4. Supplier changes
A new supplier may be added to this list only after Fleet Procurement completes a quality audit. Orders must not be placed with suppliers that are not on this list unless the Fleet Manager gives written approval.
