FLEET PROCUREMENT PROCEDURE - SPARE PARTS
Procedure PRO-12, effective 1 February 2026

1. Approval limits
Purchase orders up to 2,000 USD may be approved by the Technical Superintendent. Orders above 2,000 USD and up to 10,000 USD require the approval of the Fleet Manager. Orders above 10,000 USD require the approval of the Technical Director. An order must never be split to stay under an approval limit.

2. Quotations
For orders with a value above 5,000 USD, at least 3 written quotations must be obtained from approved suppliers. For orders of 5,000 USD or less, a single quotation from an approved supplier is sufficient.

3. Critical spares
Parts marked with criticality class C in the catalogue are critical spares: a vessel must never be without at least one on board. If the stock on board falls below the minimum level, the part must be re-ordered at once and no quotation comparison is required.

4. Emergency purchases
When a vessel is unable to sail or safety is affected, the Master or the Technical Superintendent may approve an emergency purchase by phone. The approval must be ratified in writing within 24 hours. Emergency purchases are exempt from the quotation requirement but not from the approval limits.

5. Delivery and receipt
Parts are delivered to the vessel's next port of call or to the agent nominated by the Superintendent. The receiving officer must check the delivery against the purchase order within 48 hours and report any shortage or damage to Fleet Procurement. Claims for damage in transit are not accepted after 7 days.

6. Documentation
Every purchase order must record the part number from the catalogue, the supplier code, the unit price in USD and the vessel name. Prices in other currencies must be converted at the rate published by Finance on the day the order is raised.
